# Quick Graphics Creation Guide

## 🎨 **Required Graphics for Play Store**

### **1. App Icon (512x512 PNG)**
**What you need:** A square icon representing your travel app

**Quick creation options:**
- **Canva:** https://canva.com (free templates)
- **Figma:** https://figma.com (free design tool)
- **Online icon maker:** https://www.favicon-generator.org/

**Design suggestions:**
- Use Sri Lankan colors (blue, orange, green)
- Include travel elements (compass, map, plane)
- Keep it simple and recognizable
- Text: "Smart Travel" or just "ST"

### **2. Feature Graphic (1024x500 PNG)**
**What you need:** A banner image for the Play Store

**Quick creation:**
- **Canva:** Search "Play Store feature graphic"
- **Template size:** 1024x500 pixels
- **Content:** "Smart Travel - Discover Sri Lanka"

**Design elements:**
- Beautiful Sri Lankan landscape
- App name prominently displayed
- Tagline: "Your Ultimate Travel Companion"
- Colors: Blue and orange theme

### **3. Screenshots (1080x1920 PNG)**
**What you need:** 2-8 screenshots of your app

**How to create:**
1. **Open your app** on Android device/emulator
2. **Take screenshots** of key screens:
   - Home screen with destinations
   - Trip planner interface
   - Destination details
   - Profile/settings screen
3. **Save as PNG** files
4. **Resize if needed** to 1080x1920

## 📱 **Screenshot Creation Steps**

### **Method 1: Android Device**
1. **Install your app** on Android device
2. **Open the app**
3. **Navigate to each screen**
4. **Take screenshots** (Power + Volume Down)
5. **Transfer to computer**

### **Method 2: Android Studio Emulator**
1. **Open Android Studio**
2. **Start emulator**
3. **Install your app**
4. **Take screenshots** using emulator tools
5. **Save to computer**

### **Method 3: Screen Recording**
1. **Record screen** while using app
2. **Extract frames** as images
3. **Edit and crop** as needed

## ⚡ **Quick Graphics Checklist**

- [ ] Gather reference images

### **During Creation:**
