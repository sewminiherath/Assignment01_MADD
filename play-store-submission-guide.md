# Smart Travel - Google Play Store Submission Guide

## 📱 Your App is Ready!

### ✅ Build Files Created
- **APK:** `app-release.apk` (4.3 MB)
- **AAB:** `app-release.aab` (5.8 MB) - **Recommended for Play Store**

### 📋 Submission Checklist

## Step 1: Google Play Console Setup

### 1.1 Create Developer Account
1. Go to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console)
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click **"Get started"**
4. Pay the **$25 one-time registration fee**
5. Complete developer profile verification

### 1.2 Create New App
1. Click **"Create app"**
2. Fill in app details:
   - **App name:** Smart Travel
   - **Default language:** English
   - **App or game:** App
   - **Free or paid:** Free

## Step 2: Upload App Bundle

### 2.1 Go to Release Management
1. Navigate to **"Release"** → **"Production"**
2. Click **"Create new release"**

### 2.2 Upload AAB File
1. Click **"Upload"** button
2. Select `app-release.aab` from your project
3. Wait for upload to complete
4. Add release notes:

```
🎉 Smart Travel v1.0.0 - Initial Release

✨ Features:
- Discover Sri Lanka's hidden gems
- Plan your perfect trip itinerary
- Find local restaurants and attractions
- Share your travel plans with friends
- Get personalized recommendations

🗺️ Explore 5+ major destinations:
- Anuradhapura Sacred City
- Sigiriya Rock Fortress
- Nallur Kovil Temple
- Rawana Waterfall
- Yapahuwa Fortress

🍽️ Local Dining:
- Restaurant recommendations
- Cuisine filtering
- Price range options

🔔 Smart Features:
- Real-time notifications
- Offline access
- Social sharing
- Emergency contacts

Download now and start your Sri Lankan adventure!
```

## Step 3: Complete Store Listing

### 3.1 App Information
- **App name:** Smart Travel
- **Short description:** "Discover Sri Lanka's hidden gems with Smart Travel - your ultimate travel companion"
- **Full description:** Use content from `play-store-listing-content.md`

### 3.2 Graphics (Required)
- **App icon:** 512x512 PNG (create custom icon)
- **Feature graphic:** 1024x500 PNG (create banner)
- **Screenshots:** 2-8 screenshots (1080x1920 or 1920x1080)

### 3.3 App Details
- **Category:** Travel & Local
- **Content rating:** Everyone
- **Tags:** Travel, Sri Lanka, tourism, cultural heritage

## Step 4: App Content

### 4.1 Content Rating
1. Complete content rating questionnaire
2. Select **"Everyone"** rating
3. Answer questions about app content
4. Submit for rating

### 4.2 Privacy Policy
1. Create a privacy policy (use `privacy-policy.html`)
2. Host it on your website or GitHub Pages
3. Add the URL in Play Console

### 4.3 App Access
1. Declare if your app is restricted
2. Add any necessary declarations
3. Complete data safety section

## Step 5: Pricing & Distribution

### 5.1 Pricing
- Set as **Free**
- No in-app purchases (for now)

### 5.2 Distribution
- Select countries/regions (start with Sri Lanka, India, etc.)
- Choose device categories (Phone, Tablet)
- Set up content ratings

## Step 6: Review & Publish

### 6.1 Pre-launch Checklist
- [ ] AAB uploaded successfully
- [ ] Store listing completed
- [ ] Graphics uploaded
- [ ] Content rating done
- [ ] Privacy policy added
- [ ] App signing configured

### 6.2 Submit for Review
1. Click **"Review"** button
2. Google will review your app (1-3 days)
3. You'll receive email notifications
4. Once approved, your app goes live!
