## Full Description
Smart Travel is your comprehensive travel companion for exploring the beautiful island of Sri Lanka. Whether you're planning a cultural journey through ancient cities or seeking adventure in nature's wonders, Smart Travel has everything you need.

### Key Features:
🗺️ **Smart Trip Planning**
- Create detailed itineraries with multiple destinations
- Share your travel plans with friends and family
- Get personalized recommendations based on your preferences

🏛️ **Cultural Heritage Discovery**
- Explore ancient cities like Anuradhapura and Sigiriya
- Discover sacred temples and historical sites
- Learn about Sri Lanka's rich cultural heritage

🍽️ **Local Dining Guide**
- Find the best restaurants near your destinations
- Discover authentic Sri Lankan cuisine
- Get recommendations for local specialties

📍 **Location Intelligence**
- Detailed information about 5+ major destinations
- Nearby restaurant suggestions
- Real-time location services

🔔 **Smart Notifications**
- Stay updated with travel alerts
- Get recommendations for your trip
- Emergency contact information

### Destinations Covered:

- Sigiriya Rock Fortress

- Yapahuwa Fortress
