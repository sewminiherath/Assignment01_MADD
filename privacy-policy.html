Smart Travel - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2024

Quick Summary: Smart Travel respects your privacy. We collect minimal data to provide travel recommendations and do not share your personal information with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

  • Account Information: Name, email address, mobile number (when you create an account)
  • Profile Data: Gender, address, preferences (optional)
  • Trip Data: Your travel plans, destinations, and itineraries

1.2 Location Data

  • Current Location: To provide nearby restaurant and destination recommendations
  • Location History: To improve travel suggestions (stored locally on your device)

1.3 Usage Data

  • App Usage: Features used, time spent, navigation patterns
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version
  • Analytics: Crash reports, performance data (anonymized)

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 Core App Functions

  • Provide personalized travel recommendations
  • Save and sync your trip plans across devices
  • Show nearby restaurants and attractions
  • Enable trip sharing with friends and family

2.2 App Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve app features
  • Fix bugs and enhance performance
  • Develop new features based on user needs

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Cloud Storage

  • Firebase: Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase servers
  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access Control: Only you can access your personal data

3.2 Local Storage

  • Device Storage: Some data is stored locally for offline access
  • Cache: Images and maps are cached for faster loading

4. Data Sharing

4.1 We Do NOT Share

  • Your personal information with third parties
  • Your location data with advertisers
  • Your trip plans with other users (unless you choose to share)

4.2 Limited Sharing

  • Firebase: Data is processed by Google Firebase (Google's privacy policy applies)
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics for app improvement
  • Legal Requirements: Only if required by law

5. Your Rights and Controls

5.1 Account Management

  • Update Information: Edit your profile in the app settings
  • Delete Account: Remove your account and all associated data
  • Data Export: Request a copy of your data

5.2 Location Controls

  • Location Permission: You can disable location access in device settings
  • Location History: Clear location history in app settings

6. Children's Privacy

Smart Travel is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

7. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Trip Data: Stored for 2 years after last activity
  • Analytics: Anonymous data retained for 1 year

8. International Users

If you are using Smart Travel from outside Sri Lanka, please note that your information may be transferred to and processed in Sri Lanka and other countries where our servers are located.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app and updating the "Last updated" date.
